- **Heap size misconfiguration** — the JVM `-Xmx` value may be too low for the actual workload, causing `OutOfMemoryError` under legitimate load.

At this stage, **none of these is confirmed.**

- **Traffic volume** — request rates around the time of the incident.
- **Deployment history** — was a new version released recently?
- **Host/container memory limits** and whether other processes share the host.
